Accessibility

Last updated 23 September 2026.

The standard this site aims for

This site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.2 at level AA. That covers keyboard access, colour contrast, text alternatives, heading structure, form labels and behaviour that respects your browser and system settings.

What that means here

  • Everything interactive works with a keyboard, and the focused element is always visible.
  • A skip link takes you straight to the main content.
  • Text meets AA contrast in both the light and dark themes.
  • The dark theme follows your system setting unless you choose otherwise.
  • If you have asked your system to reduce motion, transitions are removed.
  • Headings run in order, landmarks are marked up, and form fields have real labels and error messages that say what to fix.
  • No information is carried by colour alone.
  • Articles have a print stylesheet, so printing gives you the content without the navigation.

Known limitations

Booking runs through Halaxy and the Bowling Workload Tool is a separate application. I do not control the accessibility of either. If you hit a barrier in one of them, email me and I will book you in myself or work around it.

Getting to in-person consults

In-person consults happen at my clinic in Richmond. If you have access needs, email me before you book and I will sort it out.

Tell me about a problem

If part of this site is difficult to use, email thihan@thecricket.physio with what you were trying to do and what got in the way. I will fix it and tell you when it is done.
