Response times

I answer enquiries myself, usually within two business days. During tours and tournaments it takes longer. If something is urgent, call rather than email.

If you are in pain right now

This site is not a way to get clinical advice without an appointment, and I am not able to assess an injury over email. If you have severe pain, numbness, weakness, or an injury that happened suddenly and is getting worse, see a doctor or go to an emergency department rather than waiting on a reply.
