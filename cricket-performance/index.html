Most cricketers only see a physio after something hurts.

Cricket does not work neatly like that.

  • Bowling load changes.
  • Training changes.
  • Matches pile up.
  • Small problems appear.
  • Rehab needs to fit around cricket.
  • Gym work needs to fit around bowling.
  • Return to play needs more than being pain-free.

The Cricket Physio gives you a structure for managing those pieces together, whether you are injured or not.
