Where this fits

Six situations that come up often, and where each one usually starts.

Fast bowler

You are bowling more as the season approaches and want to increase load without guessing.

Usually starts with Essentials, or Performance after a back injury.

Returning from injury

Usually starts with Performance.

Recurring problem

Your back, hamstring, shoulder or knee keeps settling down, then returning once cricket load increases.

Usually starts with Performance.

Aspiring player

You want your physical preparation managed around cricket rather than following a generic gym program.

Usually starts with Integrated Performance.

Remote player

You do not live in Melbourne but want cricket-specific rehabilitation and performance oversight.

Performance and Integrated Performance both run remotely.

Uninjured player

Nothing is wrong right now. You want better monitoring and preparation before something becomes a problem.

Usually starts with Essentials.

How this differs from booking a physio when it hurts

Appointment-based physio is the right tool for plenty of problems. It is a poor tool for managing a season.

Booking when it hurts

  • You book after the problem has already cost you cricket.
  • Each appointment starts by catching up on what happened.
  • Rehab tends to stop when the pain does.
  • Your coach and S&C hear about it second-hand, if at all.
  • Gym work and bowling get planned by different people, separately.

Ongoing support

  • Workload and soreness are tracked, so change shows up earlier.
  • Your history, load and schedule are already known.
  • Rehab runs until you are prepared for the demands of cricket again.
  • From Performance up, talking to your coach, S&C and other clinicians is part of the service.
  • At Integrated Performance, gym, bowling and matches are planned together.

Neither replaces hands-on treatment when you need it. That is booked separately, and Performance and Integrated Performance members pay the member rate.

Workload monitoring

Bowling workload without spreadsheet archaeology.

You record your bowling in the Bowling Workload Tool. It keeps the history, so nobody has to dig through a notes app in March to work out what happened in November.

What gets tracked

  • Match bowling
  • Training bowling
  • Total balls
  • Soreness
  • Training status
  • Return-to-bowling progression

Ball counts do not predict injury on their own. What matters is how load changes, how it is spread across the week, how you respond the next day, and what else is going on: growth, sleep, travel, previous injury. The numbers start the conversation. They do not finish it.

Testing

Measure progress, not just pain.

Performance and Integrated Performance start with baseline physical testing, so there is something to measure change against.

Testing is adapted to your role. Fast bowlers, batters, wicketkeepers and all-rounders load their bodies differently, and testing them identically produces a tidy report that means little. Benchmarks track your change over time. They are not a pass mark.

In-person testing happens at the Richmond clinic. Remote players use field tests where practical. No screening test reliably predicts who gets injured, so none of this is sold as prediction.

Injury and return to performance

When something goes wrong

Membership does not stop injuries. It changes how early they are picked up and how well the return is planned.

Triage and diagnosis

New injury triage, imaging review and referral coordination are included at Performance and Integrated Performance. At Integrated Performance I stay involved after the specialist review. Essentials members get 20% off telehealth injury triage.

Return to performance

Rehab moves from settling symptoms to physical capacity, running, cricket movement, skills, training and matches. It finishes when you are prepared for the demands of your role, not when the pain stops.

How return to performance works

Return to bowling

Bowlers get a written progression that builds one variable at a time: volume, then intensity, then match spells. How you respond over the next 48 hours decides each step.

Reasonable use

Integrated Performance includes high-access telehealth and messaging. Performance includes telehealth follow-up for clinically relevant issues that come up between scheduled reviews. Messages get a reply within the target for your level: 72 hours at Performance, 24 hours at Integrated Performance, seven days a week.

Neither level includes:

  • Emergency medical care
  • Guaranteed same-day appointments
  • Live 24/7 communication or continuous messaging
  • Unlimited in-person treatment
  • Match-day or tournament attendance
  • Travel
  • Imaging, specialist or other external medical fees

Not an emergency service

Membership is not an emergency medical service. For a serious injury, severe or worsening pain, numbness or weakness, a head knock with any worrying signs, or chest pain, call 000 or go to an emergency department. Do not wait for a message reply.

Your data

Who sees your information

Your injury and workload data belong to you. Sharing them is your decision.

  • You see your own data. Nobody else sees it by default.
  • Coaches, S&C staff and other clinicians see it only with your permission. You choose who during onboarding.
  • Injury, rehabilitation and workload information reaches club staff only with your consent. That holds even if your club has a Club Core or Club Plus package.
  • You withdraw consent whenever you choose. Sharing stops from that point.
  • Under 18, a parent or guardian gives consent during onboarding.

Clinical records are kept under Australian health privacy law. The privacy policy explains how information is stored and who processes it.

Common questions

Do I need to be injured to join?

No. The service covers injury management, workload, physical preparation and performance. Plenty of members join with nothing wrong.

Do I need to live in Melbourne?

No. Performance and Integrated Performance support remote players through telehealth, monitoring, video review and field testing. In-person assessment and testing are available in Richmond.

Does membership include physio treatment?

Telehealth support is included according to your level. Hands-on treatment is separate, and Performance and Integrated Performance members pay 20% less for it.

What happens if I get injured?

Performance and Integrated Performance include new-injury triage and ongoing management. Essentials members get discounted telehealth triage.

Do you replace my club physio or S&C coach?

No. Where you already have a support team, this works alongside them. Good outcomes in cricket usually involve several people, and my job is to connect the pieces rather than take them over.

Do you communicate with my coach?

Integrated Performance includes ongoing communication where it helps. Performance includes communication around specific injury and return-to-play issues. Either way, only with your permission.

What happens if my scan shows an injury?

Performance and Integrated Performance include imaging review, referral coordination and communication with the clinicians involved. I do not order imaging myself; I will tell you when it is worth asking a doctor for it and why.

Do you guarantee I will not get injured?

No. Injury risk depends on many factors, several of them outside anyone's control. The service focuses on preparation, workload, picking problems up earlier, rehabilitation and better-informed decisions.

Is this only for fast bowlers?

No. It supports bowlers, batters, wicketkeepers and all-rounders. Bowling-specific monitoring naturally matters most to bowlers.

Do juniors get different pricing?

Yes. Under-18 pricing is $65, $360 or $760 a month, depending on the level. A parent or guardian completes onboarding and gives consent.

What happens if I need to pause?

Memberships pause with notice. Your place is held for pauses of up to four weeks. Longer pauses do not guarantee a place if that level is full when you come back.

Is the membership fee claimable on private health insurance?

Assume not. Health funds rebate individual consultations, and a membership fee is not itemised that way. Separate in-person appointments at Bridge Road are claimable on the spot, depending on your fund and cover. Ask your fund if you want certainty.

What if I need emergency medical care?

The membership is not an emergency medical service. Call 000 or go to an emergency department.
