Where you are going

Bridge Road Physiotherapy is my clinic. The Cricket Physio is the cricket side of the same practice, so an in-person cricket appointment is an appointment with me, at Bridge Road, booked through the Bridge Road calendar. One physiotherapist, one room, one set of notes.

Saying that plainly matters, because a patient should never be surprised about which door they walk through or whose name is on the receipt.

Why the gym floor matters for cricket

The clinic sits inside Uplift Gym, and you do not need a gym membership to attend. For cricket rehabilitation, the space is the point. Hamstrings need sprinting to be rehabilitated properly. Bowling backs need loading under fatigue. Throwing shoulders need something to throw at. A treatment room with a plinth and a resistance band does not test any of that, so people get discharged on the strength of feeling fine and break down in their second spell back.

What the first appointment covers

  • History in cricket terms: bowling volume, role, schedule, when in a spell the problem shows up.
  • Physical examination, tested against the demands of your position rather than a generic normal.
  • Hands-on treatment where it helps, which for most cricket injuries is a small part of the answer rather than the whole of it.
  • Loading on the gym floor, so we both see what you tolerate rather than guessing.
  • A written plan: what to do, what to stop, and the milestones between now and playing.
