Return to performance

From injury to performance

Pain-free is a checkpoint, not the finish line. Every return runs through six stages, and each one is earned before the next begins.

  1. Settle and understand

    Diagnosis, irritability, imaging where it changes the plan, and training modified rather than stopped.

  2. Rebuild capacity

    Strength, trunk, shoulder, lower limb and energy systems, built toward what your role demands.

  3. Reintroduce cricket

    Running, throwing, fielding and batting movements, progressed on purpose.

  4. Return to bowling

    Volume, intensity and frequency, one variable at a time, judged by the next 48 hours.

  5. Return to training

    Full training at the intensity of competition, with the response monitored.

  6. Return to performance

    Match availability, and load managed so you stay there.

The work happens between the treatment room and getting selected again.

Clinic, gym, running, throwing, nets, bowling, training, competition. Rehabilitation is built from the demands of your role, and it is tested there too.

The pillar

Fast bowling

Fast bowlers carry the heaviest injury load in the game. Most of it is decided by how bowling load changes, not by any single spell. Ball counts matter, and they are not the whole story: intensity, gaps, growth, sleep, travel and previous injury all change what the same overs do.
