Privacy policy

Last updated 23 September 2026.

This policy covers thecricket.physio and the enquiries sent through it.

Telehealth consults are booked through The Cricket Physio's own Halaxy calendar. Clinical records from telehealth consults are kept in Halaxy and protected under health records legislation.

In-person consults happen at Bridge Road Physiotherapy, which holds the clinical records for them. The Bridge Road privacy policy governs those.

What this website collects

The enquiry form on the contact page collects your name, email address, the option you choose under "I am a", your organisation if you give one, and your message. That is sent to my email inbox and is not stored by a third-party form service or written to a database by this website.

This site loads no advertising trackers, no social media pixels and no session recording. It sets no cookies of its own.

Theme preference

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is stored in your own browser using local storage so the site remembers it next time. That information stays on your device. It is not sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. No other information is shared, and Google states these requests are not used for advertising profiling.

Analytics

At the time of writing this site loads no analytics. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change is made, and any analytics used will be configured to respect your browser's privacy settings.

Links to other sites

Links to Bridge Road Physiotherapy, the Halaxy booking system and the Bowling Workload Tool take you to services with their own privacy policies. Read those for how they handle your information.

Health information

Do not send health information through the enquiry form or by email if you would rather it were handled inside a clinical record. Email is not a secure channel. Bring it to your appointment instead, where it is recorded and protected under health records legislation.

Access, correction and complaints

To ask what information is held about you, to correct it, or to make a complaint, email thihan@thecricket.physio. If you are not satisfied with the response, you are able to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
