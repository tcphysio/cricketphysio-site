Privacy policy

Last updated 24 September 2026.

This policy covers thecricket.physio and the enquiries sent through it. Clinical records for appointments are held by Bridge Road Physiotherapy, and the Bridge Road privacy policy governs those.

What this website collects

The site has four forms. Each sends what you type to my email inbox. Nothing is written to a database by this website or stored by a third-party form service.

  • Team enquiry (teams page): your name, email address, organisation and message.
  • Professional player enquiry (professional players page): your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, team, how you prefer to be contacted, whether your manager or agent is welcome to contact me, and your message. The form only sends once you tick the consent box, because a message about an injury is health information.
  • Club enquiry (club packages page): the club's name, competition level, squad size and support, what the club wants help with, and your name, role, email address and phone number. Please leave individual players' health details out of it.
  • Player membership application (player memberships page): your name, contact details, age, location, playing level and role, club, current support team, the membership you are interested in and why, and, if you choose to give it, a brief description of a current injury. For players under 18 it also asks for a parent or guardian's name and email address. The form only sends once you tick the consent box, and the information is used to work out which membership suits you and to contact you about it.

Form submissions are relayed to my inbox by Resend, an email delivery service based in the United States, so the message passes through servers outside Australia on the way.

This site loads no advertising trackers, no social media pixels and no session recording. It sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser.

Fonts

Typefaces are served from this website. Your browser makes no request to Google or any other font service.

Analytics

At the time of writing this site loads no analytics. It names certain interactions, such as a click on a booking button, inside the page itself so that an analytics tool added later picks them up; at present nothing is sent anywhere. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change is made, and any analytics used will be configured to respect your browser's privacy settings.

Links to other sites

Links to Bridge Road Physiotherapy, the Halaxy booking system and the Bowling Workload Tool take you to services with their own privacy policies. Read those for how they handle your information.

Health information

The player application is the only form that asks about health, and only briefly. Keep it short: the detailed history is taken at your assessment, where it is recorded in a clinical record and protected under health records legislation. Email is not a fully secure channel, so do not send more than the form asks for, and do not send health information through the team or club forms.

Members

If you become a member, the bowling, soreness and training information you record is held in the Bowling Workload Tool, which has its own privacy policy. Who else sees it, such as a coach, an S&C coach or a club, is decided by the consent you give during onboarding, and you withdraw that consent whenever you choose. For players under 18, a parent or guardian gives consent. The player memberships page sets this out in plain terms.

Access, correction and complaints

To ask what information is held about you, to correct it, or to make a complaint, email thihan@thecricket.physio. If you are not satisfied with the response, you are able to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
