Working with your team

Alongside your medical staff, never around them.

Most professional players already have good people around them. My job is to add a cricket-specific second view and make the handover clean.

  1. A short call

    Arranged around your schedule and time zone. What is going on, what has been done, and what decision is coming up.

  2. Review

    History, imaging, reports and footage. With your consent, I speak to your team doctor or physio directly.

  3. A written plan

    Clear criteria and milestones, written in the language a team medical department works in.

  4. Follow-through

    Reviews as the return progresses, and a clean handover back to your team when you rejoin them.

Common questions

Will you tell my team?

Only if you want me to. Many players want their team doctor or physio involved, and I am happy to speak to them directly. That is your decision, not mine.

Do you replace my team's medical staff?

No. I add a cricket-specific second view and a clear plan. The best outcomes come from your team and outside input pulling in the same direction.

I'm overseas. How does it work across time zones?

By video, at a time that suits your schedule rather than mine. Plans are written so a local physio is able to deliver the hands-on work.

Is it possible to see you in person?

Yes, in Melbourne, at Bridge Road Physiotherapy in Richmond, which has a gym floor for proper loading and testing.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Nothing about you appears anywhere without your written permission, and clinical records are kept under Australian health privacy law.
