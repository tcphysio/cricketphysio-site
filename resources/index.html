A note on these guides

Dated, referenced, and revised

Clinical thinking changes, and a page written three years ago and never revisited should not be presented as current. Every guide shows when it was last reviewed, and cites its sources. If a guide is out of date, the date says so rather than the page pretending otherwise.

Reading about it is a start.
