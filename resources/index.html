I talk about cricket injuries, return to play and how high performance departments are structured. For teaching, podcasts or conference sessions, email me with the audience, the format and the date.

Recent appearances include the Physio Foundations podcast, on injury, surgery and rehabilitation from the patient's side.

For clinicians

If you are a physiotherapist seeing cricketers and want a second opinion on a bowling back, a return-to-bowling progression, or how an SSSM department fits together, that is a conversation I am happy to have. Get in touch.

A note on these guides

Everything here carries a date it was last reviewed. Clinical thinking changes, and a page written three years ago and never revisited should not be presented as current. If a guide is out of date, the date says so rather than the page pretending otherwise.
