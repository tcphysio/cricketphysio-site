Consulting work

What an assessment involves

The first appointment is about working out three things: what the problem is, what has been driving it, and what has to change.

History, in cricket terms

How much you have bowled, batted and thrown, and how that has changed recently. What your role in the side is. What the next six weeks of your schedule looks like. When the pain shows up in a spell, and what happens the next morning. This part does most of the diagnostic work.

Physical examination

Movement, strength, control and tolerance, tested against what your role demands rather than against a generic normal. A bowler's requirements differ from a keeper's.

A plan you leave with

In writing. What to do, what to stop, what the milestones are, and what has to be true before the next step. If imaging or a doctor's opinion is needed, I will say so and explain why.

What this is not

I do not run injury prediction screening. No test battery reliably predicts who gets hurt, and selling one would be dishonest. Testing here is used to set a baseline and measure change, which is useful and is a smaller claim.

I do not prescribe medication or order imaging. Where either is warranted, you need a doctor, and I will tell you what to ask for.

Not sure which one you need

Describe the problem and I will tell you honestly whether it needs an examination, a video call, or neither.
