The first appointment

What an assessment involves

  1. History, in cricket terms

    How much you have bowled, batted and thrown, and how that changed recently. Your role, the next six weeks of fixtures, when the pain shows up and what happens the next morning.

  2. Examination against your role

    Movement, strength, control and tolerance, tested against what your position demands rather than a generic normal. A bowler's requirements differ from a keeper's.

  3. A plan you leave with

    In writing. What to do, what to stop, the milestones, and what has to be true before the next step.

What this is not

Honest limits

I do not run injury prediction screening. No test battery reliably predicts who gets hurt. Testing here sets a baseline and measures change, which is useful and is a smaller claim.

I do not prescribe medication or order imaging. Where either is warranted, you need a doctor, and I will tell you what to ask for.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the problem and I will tell you honestly whether it needs an examination, a video call, or neither.
